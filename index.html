4 · Same roof, every border state

The roof and inputs above, re-run under each utility serving a state bordering Nebraska. Nebraska (LES) is the home baseline. Battery demand-shaving is worth more where demand charges are high — and sunnier states produce more from the same roof.

Planning estimates only — the border-state tariffs are blended planning values, not quoted rates. Export is valued at $0.03/kWh in every state; states with retail net metering would value exports higher. State incentives are not modeled. Payback beyond the 25-year horizon is shown as 25.

Assumptions

    Independent concept tool. Not affiliated with MARS Energy Group, Nelnet, or any utility. Rates, yields, and tax treatment are planning estimates — verify with the serving utility and a tax advisor before committing capital. ITC and MACRS treatment reflects the Inflation Reduction Act as generally applied to commercial solar; project-specific eligibility (prevailing wage, apprenticeship, domestic content, energy community) must be confirmed.
